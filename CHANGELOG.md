# Farm Counter

## v1.0.0

- First release.
- Counts fish and profession loot: herbs, ore, leather, cloth, gems and pearls (prospecting), enchanting materials (disenchanting), elemental, pigments, parts/salvage and other reagents.
- Only items you actually loot are counted - no bag moves, purchases, mail or crafting.
- Auction house values via TradeSkillMaster (optional): per item, per category, session total and gold per hour. Price source configurable with `/farmcounter price`.
- Sessions with start/stop, the last 5 sessions in a small dropdown, and a global history.
- Close button in the window; `/farmcounter show` brings it back.
- Options page under Game Menu > Options > AddOns > Farm Counter (`/farmcounter options`): sounds, rare (blue) sound on/off, counting, price source and categories.
- Own addon icon and listed under "Professions" in the AddOns list.
- Optional auto hide (off by default): hides the window after 2 minutes (adjustable) without counted loot and shows it again with the next counted loot.
